Moving Platforms & Swap Resources

Try two mechanics adapted from the C# scripts in my university team project.

Standalone browser adaptation of selected mechanics. It uses simple shapes and does not run the team's original PainterEngine game.

1. Moving platforms

Each platform travels between two endpoints, reverses direction, and repeats. Different starting phases stagger their movement.

45 units/s

Platforms running.

2. Replenish a limited swap resource

Move the blue player onto the gold pickup, then press E or select Collect. Each press adds one swap, up to four.

Swaps: 2 / 4

Move right to reach the pickup.

Keyboard: A/D or arrow keys move; E collects while touching; C refills for testing. “Use one swap” isolates resource consumption; character swapping is not simulated here. The test starts at two swaps so replenishment is easy to observe.

Implementation reference: VerticalMovingPlatform.cs, ExtraSwapItem.cs and CheatCode.cs. The original scripts run in PainterEngine; this page adapts their movement and resource rules to JavaScript.
